Our Mission

Explore Mars through real planetary science.

We bridge orbital topography, planetary stratigraphy, and rover-level surface photography so students, researchers, and enthusiasts can cross-reference Martian landmarks in one workflow.

1. Discover Locations

Navigate notable volcanoes, ancient lake basins, and tectonic canyons using elevation models and coordinate metadata linked directly to NASA Mars Trek.

2. Understand Geology

Cross-check surface units, Amazonian lava flows, and Noachian clay-bearing strata with USGS–NASA Planetary Geologic Mapping standards.

3. See the Surface

Inspect camera subsystems and jump straight into NASA's Mars 2020 Perseverance Raw Images repository to examine uncalibrated surface frames.

Authoritative Data Sources

Explore trusted Mars science portals.

MARTIAN is built around open planetary science archives maintained by NASA, JPL, and the U.S. Geological Survey.

🛰️ NASA / JPL

NASA JPL Mars Trek

An interactive web-based planetary mapping and modeling portal allowing users to visualize surface terrain, measure elevation profiles, generate 3D print files, and overlay multi-mission science layers.

About MARTIAN

Built by Team Callistro.

Created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, MARTIAN demonstrates how open orbital GIS tools and rover telemetry can be unified into an intuitive learning and exploration interface.

Scientific Transparency

Every visual schematic clearly distinguishes illustrative summaries from primary NASA/JPL and USGS datasets so users develop strong data literacy.

Extensible Data Pipeline

Designed to pair seamlessly with Python, Pandas, and Streamlit analytics workflows for querying Mars 2020 JSON feeds and WMTS map tiles.

Accessible Exploration

Lowering the barrier to planetary science by giving anyone one-click access to real Martian topography and rover cameras.
